About

Now · August 2026

Just finished my PhD at Imperial and continue to work on topics around robustness verification for perception models and validation for AI agents. Currently most interested in scaling verification to production-size models and exploring which ideas from verification of vision models transfer to LLMs and AI agents.

I'm an AI research scientist at Safe Intelligence in London, working on formal verification and certified robustness of neural networks and AI agents. Most of what I do is about getting verification out of the paper and into contact with real models: verifiers for object detectors, certified training methods, and end-to-end robustness assessments for customers.

I finished my PhD in Computer Science at Imperial College London in 2026, supervised by Alessio Lomuscio, on the formal verification and certified training of neural networks. Before that I studied Industrial Engineering and Management at KIT in Karlsruhe, with a semester abroad in Glasgow, and drifted towards machine learning somewhere in the middle of it.

Away from the desk I sail, ride mountain bikes and play tennis.
